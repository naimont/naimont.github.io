Agricultural Water Treatment and FSMA Compliance

← Back to Project Portfolio

Project Summary

Evaluated sanitizer efficacy in preharvest agricultural water collected from Florida farms under realistic conditions. Verified performance of chlorine-based sanitizers across different contact times, temperatures, and sanitizer concentrations. Applied kinetic modeling to estimate the treatment conditions required to achieve FSMA’s 3-log microbial reduction target, supporting risk-based water management decisions for produce safety. In related work, evaluated novel treatment approaches such as chitosan microparticles, which reduced microbial loads in fecally polluted water by up to 2 log.

Why It Matters

Agricultural water quality varies substantially across production environments, which directly affects treatment efficacy and food safety outcomes. This work addresses that challenge by evaluating microbial control strategies under realistic water conditions rather than idealized laboratory matrices.

The results are directly relevant to produce operations and regulatory compliance, as they identify treatment conditions required to achieve FSMA-aligned microbial reduction targets under variable field conditions. This supports validation of agricultural water treatment strategies and implementation of risk-based preventive controls.

In multiple cases, viral targets demonstrated greater resistance than bacterial indicators under comparable treatment conditions, highlighting limitations of indicator-based validation approaches. This underscores the importance of integrating experimental data with kinetic modeling to inform risk-based water management decisions that account for variability in microbial responses.

Key Contributions

  • Evaluated sanitizer efficacy in agricultural water collected from commercial farm environments
  • Quantified treatment performance across different contact times, temperatures, and concentrations
  • Applied kinetic modeling to estimate treatment conditions required to achieve FSMA’s 3-log microbial reduction target
  • Assessed novel mitigation approaches, including chitosan microparticles, for microbial reduction in fecally polluted water
  • Generated data to support risk-based agricultural water management decisions
